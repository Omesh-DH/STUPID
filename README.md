# STUPID - Self-Therapy for Unmotivated People In Denial

![STUPID Logo](https://img.shields.io/badge/STUPID-Self--Therapy%20for%20Unmotivated%20People%20In%20Denial-blueviolet)

**STUPID** is a satirical digital health/wellness app that parodies corporate wellness culture, the self-help industry, and motivational content. It looks aesthetically pleasing like Calm, Headspace, or Noom (soft blues, greens, rounded corners, friendly typography) but delivers aggressively dark, dad-joke-filled, brutally honest content.

## 🎯 Core Brand Message

> **"If you're an idiot, that's not my problem."**

## 🎨 Visual Aesthetic

- Fake wellness app design (gradient backgrounds, serene nature images, smiling diverse people)
- Professional UI/UX that looks legitimate at first glance
- Hidden dark humor in microcopy, error messages, and loading screens

## 🎭 Target Audience

- People who hate wellness culture
- Corporate employees forced into "wellness programs"
- Gen Z/Millennials with dark humor
- Anyone who enjoys passive-aggressive positivity

## 😈 Tone

- Passive-aggressive positivity
- Wasabi-in-cake energy
- Corporate gaslighting meets stand-up comedy
- Brutally honest with a smile

## 🚀 Features

### 🧘 Meditation
- **Ocean Waves**: Listen to soothing sounds while pretending you're on vacation
- **Forest Bathing**: Immerse yourself in nature sounds from your apartment
- **Guided Visualization**: Let our soothing voice guide you to a place that's not your current reality
- **Binaural Beats**: Scientifically proven to do something impressive-sounding
- **Sleep Meditation**: Fall asleep while someone whispers nonsense
- **Quick Meditation**: For people with attention spans shorter than a TikTok

### 💪 Motivation
- **Daily Motivation Generator™**: Get fresh doses of empty inspiration
- **Quote Categories**: Career, Love, Fitness, Success, Humor, Personal Growth
- **30-Day Challenges**: Commit to daily motivation, fail by day 3
- **Success Stories**: Read about how other people changed their lives (they didn't)
- **Motivation Tools**: Goal Tracker, Progress Charts, Productivity Timer, etc.

### 📊 Progress Tracking
- **Failure Dashboard**: Beautiful visualization of your inability to follow through
- **Goal Management**: Set goals you won't achieve, track progress you won't make
- **Charts & Graphs**: Watch as your motivation flatlines in real-time
- **Insights**: AI-powered analysis of your failure patterns

### 📖 About
- **Our Mission**: To provide beautiful, expensive ways to avoid dealing with actual problems
- **Our Story**: How a simple idea became a multi-million dollar way to waste time
- **Our Team**: Meet the team that doesn't care
- **Our Philosophy**: The guiding principles behind our complete lack of principles
- **Press**: As seen in our imaginations

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript
- **Charts**: Chart.js
- **Design**: Custom CSS with CSS Variables, Gradients, Animations
- **Storage**: LocalStorage (for goal tracking)
- **No Frameworks**: Pure, unadulterated HTML/CSS/JS

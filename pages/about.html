Our Story

How a simple idea became a multi-million dollar way to waste time.

2020

The Spark of an Idea

Our founder, while avoiding actual work, had a vision: What if there was a wellness app that looked legitimate but was actually just a fancy way to procrastinate? STUPID was born.

2021

Development Begins

We spent months designing beautiful interfaces and writing clever microcopy. The actual functionality was an afterthought. It still is.

2022

Beta Launch

We launched our beta to a select group of friends and family. They all said it was great. They were lying. We launched anyway.

2023

Viral Growth

Thanks to a clever marketing campaign that preyed on people's insecurities, STUPID went viral. Millions of people signed up to track their failure.

2024

The Present

Today, STUPID is used by millions of people worldwide who are all successfully avoiding their problems in style. Our users report feeling exactly the same as before they started using our app. Mission accomplished.

Our Philosophy

The guiding principles behind our complete lack of principles.

🤷

Embrace the Absurd

Life has no inherent meaning. Neither does this app. And that's okay. We're all just here to pass the time until we die. Might as well have a nice-looking interface while we do it.

💸

Capitalize on Insecurity

People are insecure. They want to be better. They'll pay for the illusion of improvement. We provide that illusion at a premium price. It's the American dream, baby.

🎭

Fake It Till You Make It

We don't have to actually help people. We just have to make them think we're helping them. With enough good design and clever marketing, no one will notice we're full of it.

😂

Humor as a Coping Mechanism

If you can't fix the problem, laugh at it. If you can't laugh at it, ignore it. If you can't ignore it, pretend it doesn't exist. That's basically our entire business model.

🌈

Aesthetic Over Function

Why build something that works when you can build something that looks like it works? Our users don't care if it's functional. They just want to feel like they're doing something productive.

🚀

Growth at Any Cost

We don't care about ethics, morality, or actually helping people. We care about growth, metrics, and making money. If that means preying on people's insecurities, so be it. Business is business.
